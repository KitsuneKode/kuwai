## Remove

- `~/Scripts/voxtype-esc-guard.sh`: uses `hyprctl keyword`, gone in Hyprland
  0.57; not running; the Esc bind in `hypr/kuwai/voxtype.lua` replaced it.
- `voxtype-3day-check.timer`: a one-time reminder for 2026-10-30, elapsed.
- 11 merged worktrees under `.worktrees/` (stable aside): their branches are in
  main (`git worktree list`; `git branch --merged`).
- `kuwai-hyde-waybar` and `waybar_start`, once the island is the only bar.
- `refs/` is 2.4 GB of other projects' clones for reading; keep only the ones
  still being read.

## Odd, and why

- **fastfetch looked broken in a half-width pane**: HyDE's config (its
  `hyprctl splash` prints just "h" under the Lua config; 44-column boxes and
  long rows wrap; its logo is a kitty image at fixed cells). kuwai's config
  replaces it (`kuwai-home`).
- **pocket-dots' animated fetch pictures** are PokeAPI's Pokémon sprites,
  downloaded by its installer (not in its repo, and its code is GPL).
  `kuwai-fetch --get-pokemon 1-151` or `random=10` fetches the same kind for
  this machine.
- **Esc did nothing in the launcher while dictating**: Hyprland's global Esc
  bind armed voxtype's double-press cancel first. Fixed (keys file).

## Mouse and keyboard, next

1. Every row that opens something opens on one click (done for notifications;
   check Agents, T3, Updates and Launcher against it).
2. Right-click everywhere a row has more than one thing to do (ContextMenu):
   Agents rows (Allow, Deny, Jump), T3 rows (Open, Copy link, Dismiss).
3. Esc always steps back one level, never closes two; Back on every sub-view.
4. Hover never hides what you are reading; hover-only controls get a keyboard
   path (KeyPath) and a right-click entry.
5. Scrolling: wheel over a capsule cycles what it shows; wheel over a slider
   moves it (volume and brightness already do).
