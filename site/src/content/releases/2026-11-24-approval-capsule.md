Approvals and agent questions previously tinted the entire agents indicator orange, blending prompts that needed an answer with threads still working quietly in the background.

Waiting prompts now sit in a dedicated orange capsule closest to the notch on the left. Click once to jump straight to the waiting pane or answer directly through native permission hooks. The right-side limits capsule now leads with whichever provider resets soonest and separates other exhausted accounts behind a subtle divider.
