- **Separate login session:** Installs into `~/.config/kuwai/hypr/` without modifying `~/.config/hypr`. `./install.sh --uninstall` restores files and systemd user units recorded before the first install.
- **The island:** One top-edge notch with balanced capsules for agents, usage limits, system news, and minimized windows, plus hover glance, privacy dots, timers, calendar, media, and keyboard-answerable cards.
- **Notifications, controls, and launcher:** Built-in `org.freedesktop.Notifications` server with sensitive-app privacy, opt-in GitHub/Gmail feeds, per-app PipeWire sound mixer, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/Display controls, and a launcher covering apps, windows, files, math, processes/ports (`!`), and Arch/AUR packages (`+`).
- **Clipboard, lock, and login:** Opt-in clipboard shelf (`Super+V`) with Quick Look, OCR, pins, and secret masking; shared five-dot password pill and hold-to-power actions on the session lock and optional `greetd` login screen.
- **Standalone themes and screensavers:** `kuwai look` with 13 dark theme definitions (wallpapers downloaded on demand and credited in `themes/*/CREDITS.txt`), notch-origin wallpaper transitions, live wallpapers, and five screensaver modes.
- **Agents, usage, cleanup, and updates:** Unified Agents and Usage sections with native permission hooks, unused-skill and cache cleanup (Trash-first), and detached system updates with AUR PKGBUILD diff review and `.pacnew` handling.
- **Stable channel and signed updates:** Session runs from `~/.local/share/kuwai` under `kuwai-shell.service`; `kuwai update-self` verifies release tags against `release-signers`, runs migrations, and rolls back on failure.
