Kuwai Night: the pictures this theme downloads, and whose they are.
kuwai does not ship them: `install.sh`, `kuwai-look` and `kuwai-theme install` fetch each from
Wikimedia Commons and verify its SHA-256 digest before use. Each stays under its own licence;
none was changed except scaled to fit a screen when installed through `kuwai-theme`.
