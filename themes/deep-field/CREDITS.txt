Deep Field: the pictures and films this theme downloads, and whose they are.
kuwai does not ship them: `kuwai-theme install` fetches each from Wikimedia Commons and checks it
is the file listed here. Each stays under its own licence; none was changed except scaled to fit
a screen (and, for films, re-encoded to loop).
